# Roamorrow Web

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Solo travelers choosing something concrete to do nearby, often on a phone while already outside.

## Product Purpose

Open the map, compare experiences, read their sources and practical details, then choose walking directions. The traveler makes the decision.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Experience is the core unit. Coordinates stay longitude, latitude.
- Browsing does not require an account or an AI prompt. Ask is optional and opens explicitly.
- Source confidence, unknown walk times, sample data, offline states and recoverable errors remain visible.
- Location and microphone access require an explicit action; closing Ask stops listening and preserves the typed draft.
- Travel rituals and iPhone concepts are Preview illustrations, not available subscription benefits.

## Brand Commitments

Roamorrow. Travel paper and a map that receives the main attention, as explicitly requested in GET-284. Preserve existing brand assets and truthful copy.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed scope: GET-284 and AGENTS.md. Product behavior: src/app/app/page.tsx and its map-first browser acceptance suite. Existing product background: ../../docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md. Live references: https://roamorrow.com and the supplied Ambi direction; Ambi is an aesthetic reference, not evidence of Roamorrow capabilities.

## Product Principles

The map leads. Experiences explain what to do. AI offers supported choices. Preview remains secondary.
